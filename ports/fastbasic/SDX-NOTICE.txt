SpartaDOS X utilities accompanying the FastBasic workflow

EDX, MD5 and ARC are the original binaries selected by Exec816's SDX input
manifests, with version-specific adaptations applied in RAM when loaded.
They retain their original authorship and distribution terms.

EDX: EDitor for SpartaDOS X, (c) 2017 Mono/Tristesse.
Original EDX archive and manual: SpartaDOS X 4.51 Toolkit,
https://sdx.atari8.info/sdx_files/4.51/toolkit.atr

MD5 and ARC: SpartaDOS X Toolkit/cartridge distributions.
Input locations, checksums and version records are in the accompanying source:
  exec816/docs/plans/spartados-personality-inputs.json
  exec816/docs/development/sdx-edx-audit.json
  exec816/compat/sdx/

The Exec816 GPL/MIT licence map does not relicense these original utilities.
