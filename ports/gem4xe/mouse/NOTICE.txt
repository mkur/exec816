Selected files: tools/gemdata.py and the pointer expansion in src/vdi/dev_vbxe.c.
Exact source and selection hashes: ports/gem4xe/mouse/inputs.json in Exec816.

The eight MFORMs are transcribed by GEM4XE from EmuTOS aes/mforms.c
(GPLv2, generated there from mform.rsc). The GEM4XE module retains this
provenance. Exec816 selects all eight forms into readonly upper memory and
adapts mask/data expansion to its existing display ownership and fences.

See GEM-COPYING.txt, GEM-COPYING.LIB.txt and GEM-LICENSING.md in this
distribution for the donor's licence notices. No donor startup, polling or
scheduling implementation is included in this mouse selection.
